How it works

Not flashcards.
Real phonics.

The Science of Reading shows that children build permanent reading memory by connecting sounds to letters — not memorizing word shapes. Every session in this app follows that method exactly.

01
See the heart part

Every word shows the irregular heart part underlined in red. Your child sees exactly which letters are tricky and which ones follow the rules.

02
Tap the sound boxes

One box per sound. Your child taps each Elkonin box while saying the sound out loud. This multisensory connection is what builds memory.

03
Blend back to the word

After tapping all sounds, blend them back into the whole word. The magic formula: Say it → Tap it → Blend it. Repeat three times.

The heart word method

Why we call them
Heart Words

Most of a word can be sounded out. Only the small irregular part needs to be memorized — learned by heart. The red underline shows your child exactly which letters that is.

the
"the" — heart part: E
The E makes an unexpected /u/ sound. Tap /th/ then /u/.
was
"was" — two heart parts: A and S
The A says /u/ and the S says /z/. Two sneaky letters.
of
"of" — both letters are heart parts
O says /u/ and F says /v/. Every letter is irregular.
and
"and" — no heart parts at all
Fully regular. /a/ then /n/ then /d/. Just tap and blend.

When your child masters a word it moves to a visible pile. Watching it grow is one of the most motivating experiences for a young reader.
