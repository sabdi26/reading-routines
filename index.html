Why this is happening

The reading system taught
your child to guess.

Many schools still teach children to recognise whole word shapes and guess using pictures — not to decode sounds. When this method fails — and for millions of kids it does — the child blames themselves.

Problem 01
Sight word memorisation
Teaching kids to recognise word shapes instead of decoding them phonetically. Works for simple words, collapses fast with anything unfamiliar.
Problem 02
Picture-guessing strategies
Children are taught to look at pictures and guess the word. This feels like reading. It isn't — and it creates a ceiling they hit hard around grade 2 or 3.
Problem 03
No early diagnostic
Schools wait until children are significantly behind before evaluating. By then, the child has spent years feeling like the problem is with them — not the method.
The Reading Routines diagnostic

Every struggling reader has
one gap. Let's find yours.

The science of reading identifies four foundational skills. When one is missing, nothing clicks — no matter how many apps you try. Our free diagnostic tells you which one in 10 minutes.

01
Phonemic Awareness
Hearing and manipulating individual sounds in spoken words. The root skill before phonics can begin — and the most commonly missed gap in early readers.
can't rhyme, struggles to blend sounds out loud
02
Phonics & Decoding
Connecting letters to sounds and blending into words. Without this, children guess at words using pictures — exactly what the school system taught them to do.
sounds out first letters then guesses the whole word
03
Sight Word Fluency
Automatically recognising high-frequency words. Gaps here make reading exhausting — every line is a battle of effort with no payoff or enjoyment.
reads slowly, loses meaning, gives up mid-page
04
Reading Fluency
Reading accurately at pace with expression. Fluency is the bridge between decoding words and actually understanding — and loving — what you read.
reads every word correctly but can't retell the story
